If you don't have a member tag yet, you're in the right place.

**Before you join camp**
1. Read #syndicate-rules
2. Change your Discord nickname to the toon you'll camp with
3. Click **I Agree** below — that accepts the rules and grants your member tag

**Already from a merge?**
If you were part of the Kingdom merge or the CA/SS merge and still need converted points, request them in #point-adjustments once you can see it (it unlocks with your tag).

**Camp while waiting on perms?**
You can still use `/ds start` and `/ds stop` to earn points until your tag is granted.
